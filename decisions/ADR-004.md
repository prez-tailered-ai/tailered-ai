# ADR-004: Treat Hermes and Honcho as reference architectures, not dependencies

## Context

An evidence-first audit assessed NousResearch/hermes-agent (MIT, `ed5e17f4b86da0c4f09c0694757b6074ae6b9d16`) and plastic-labs/honcho (AGPL-3.0, `a92fb1e0789fd29e9674aec133328513ed0dcda3`) as candidate contributors to Tailered AI. The target repository was frozen at `6172653e0aca0981d0abaf4ad8e9d587667737e9`. Neither upstream repository was modified, pushed to, or forked. The corpus is `docs/audits/hermes-honcho/`, and its dispositions are 0 ADOPT, 0 REPLACE, 1 ADAPT, 1 INTEROPERATE under mandatory gates, 7 REFERENCE, 4 DEFER, and 7 REJECT.

Executed proof established two facts. The Tailered process boundary contains agent mutation and accounting, and does not contain execution. The Tailered ledger is not concurrency-safe under parallel runs, and a started run can produce no terminal `EvalRow`.

An adoption decision is intent, so the audit drafted this decision and did not append it. This is the founder decision. The audit's draft is revised here so that it establishes direction and gates and does not read as implementation authorization.

## Decision

Treat Hermes Agent and Honcho as reference architectures. Neither is a dependency of Tailered AI.

Adopt no upstream component, package, runtime, library, service, or hosted endpoint through this decision.

Tailered may later implement Tailered-native adaptations of selected upstream ideas. Each adaptation requires its own approved implementation gate and its own evidence. This decision grants no such gate.

The memory-provider seam, fail-open bounded prefetch, compare-and-swap task ownership, the procedure format, and procedure-outcome measurement remain roadmap items. None is an authorized implementation.

Honcho remains DEFERRED. Honcho may be reconsidered only as an isolated external service, never as linked source, and only after every technical, security, tenancy, deletion, licensing, cost, and evaluation gate passes.

Ledger concurrency safety is mandatory before any multi-agent execution.

This decision authorizes no deployment.

## Alternatives rejected

- Accept the audit's draft verbatim. Its wording directs reimplementation of three ideas and adoption of a procedure format, which reads as immediate implementation authorization and would bypass the per-adaptation gate.
- Adopt Hermes as the Tailered process agent now. It has no reserve-before-spend, and it publishes no installable artifact, so the fourth operating law could not be honoured.
- Adopt the Hermes autonomous skill writer. Its learning loop is unmeasured, so the capability cannot be shown to help and cannot be governed by evidence.
- Use Honcho as a store of record. Derived belief must never enter canonical state.
- Defer this decision until the roadmap gates complete. Direction would stay unrecorded while implementation pressure accumulated against no stated posture.

## Consequences

- Tailered keeps its zero-dependency runtime, its executable definition of done, and its single write chokepoint.
- Every upstream-inspired capability arrives through its own gate, with its own evidence, or it does not arrive.
- Parallelism is gated behind ledger concurrency safety rather than assumed.
- Any future Honcho integration carries an AGPL network-interaction boundary and requires counsel review.
- The audit corpus becomes durable architectural reference rather than an adoption backlog.
- Roadmap work that cites this decision as authorization is out of order, and the citation is invalid.
